# Project Context — Industrial HR & Labour Compliance Consultancy Website

## 1. Project Overview

Build a professional, modern, trust-focused informational and lead-generation website for a future consultancy focused on:

- Industrial HR
- Labour and statutory compliance
- PF / EPFO compliance support
- ESIC compliance support
- Payroll and HR operations
- Factory / industrial establishment compliance assistance
- Contract labour compliance
- Industrial relations
- HR documentation and policies
- Compliance audits / health checks
- Labour inspection and notice support, within the legally permitted scope
- Coordination with appropriately qualified legal, accounting, company-secretarial, safety, and other professionals where specialist services are required

The website should position the firm as a serious B2B consultancy for factories, MSMEs, contractors, and workforce-intensive businesses.

The website is NOT a recruitment-agency website and should NOT be positioned primarily around talent acquisition.

---

## 2. Business Positioning

### Core positioning

Industrial HR & Labour Compliance Consultancy

### Core promise

Help businesses build better HR processes, maintain statutory compliance, identify compliance gaps, and manage workforce-related operational risks.

### Primary target perception

When a factory owner, HR manager, admin manager, or MSME owner visits the website, they should think:

> "These people understand industrial HR, labour compliance, and workforce operations."

They should NOT think:

> "This is just another recruitment consultancy."

## 17. Legal / Compliance Content Rules

The website operates around labour, HR and statutory compliance.

Therefore:

- Avoid presenting general website information as legal advice.
- Clearly state that information is general/educational unless reviewed for a specific engagement.
- Do not imply the firm is a law firm unless it legally is one.
- Do not claim advocate services unless appropriately qualified and authorized.
- Do not claim CA/CS/safety-professional services unless provided by qualified professionals.
- Use "assistance", "support", "coordination", "consulting", or similar accurate language where appropriate.
- Legal disclaimers should be reviewed before launch by an appropriate professional.

## 20. Important Product Principle

The website should sell an outcome, not paperwork.

Weak positioning:
"We file PF and ESIC returns."

Better:
"We help businesses manage recurring statutory compliance and maintain accurate workforce records."

Weak:
"We provide labour consultancy."

Better:
"We help factories and MSMEs identify, organize and manage their HR and labour compliance requirements."
